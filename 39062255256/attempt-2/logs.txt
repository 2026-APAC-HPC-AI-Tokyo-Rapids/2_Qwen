1/1 is the restructured loop with no cross-unit pipelining: N_SWAP is
selected once per block and the whole K loop runs inside the
specialization, instead of re-dispatching through the 8-way switch on
every K block. It was the fastest variant at every Decode size (4 to 128
tokens, -2.9% to -11.6% vs the image), and adding the two-deep pipeline
on top made every size slower. The instrumented timelines explain why: the
pipelined K loop itself is a few percent shorter, but the unrolled chunk
bodies of the eight N_SWAP specializations evict the epilogue and loop
code from the instruction cache (FC2 epilogue per CTA 9 -> 14-16 us,
widening per-CTA spread), and the promotion/barrier work hidden by the
pipeline is a small part of the per-K-block cost. Only chunked pipelines
are possible at all: ptxas serializes every WGMMA of the kernel (C7514)
when a WGMMA is in flight across a loop back-edge and its accumulator is
read afterwards.

This commit replaces the pipelined candidate with that 1/1 structure
written directly (patches/deep-gemm-bench/sm90-decode-swap-ab-hoist.patch):
no chunk machinery, no second accumulator buffer for L1, no
CUTLASS_LAMBDA_FUNC_INLINE (the smaller body inlines on its own; SASS is
identical with and without it), weight-scale rows computed once per block
and loaded before the full-barrier wait, the image's per-K-block order
(L1: issue, wait<0>, promote, arrive; L2: low and high groups, wait<1>,
promote low, wait<0>, promote high, arrive). Decode: 168 registers, 0
spills, no C7510/C7514, cubin 135 KB (image 120 KB); Prefill SASS
byte-identical to the image. Its code layout differs slightly from the
1/1 chunk variant, so this run is both the measurement of the final patch
and a repeat of the hoist result.

The timeline patch is rebased on this header (the two full-barrier waits
of the new branch use tl_wait_full) and keeps the ML1Loop/ML2Loop
counters.
