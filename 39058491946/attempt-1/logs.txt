2026-10-17T18:31:53.7330805Z   QWEN_COMMIT_MESSAGE: [cluster:firmus] [nodes:1] [kernel-bench] Software-pipeline the SwapAB Decode MegaMoE mainloop (chunks of 8 L1 / 6 L2 K blocks)

Motivation: the kernel timeline of runs 39053820377/39054922367
(research/runtime-performance/2026-10-18-megamoe-kernel-timeline.md) shows
Decode-128 MegaMoE spending ~220 us in GEMM while weights stream at only
~2.7 TB/s, the math warpgroups almost never wait for a full stage and the TMA
loaders wait 150-190 us for empty slots. The math warpgroups' per-K-block
chain (full wait -> SwapAB WGMMA -> warpgroup_wait<0> -> FP32 promote with
per-token scales -> empty arrive) is serial, so nothing overlaps the WGMMA
latency of one K block with the promotion and barrier traffic of the
previous one.

Candidate (patches/deep-gemm-bench/sm90-decode-mainloop-pipeline.patch,
applied over the image's DeepGEMM; precision-neutral):
- New kSwapABActive branch of process_math_block. N_SWAP is selected once
  per block and the whole K loop runs inside the specialization, so two
  swap accumulators stay in registers across K blocks.
- L1: K block k is issued into accum[k % 2]; after warpgroup_wait<1> the
  previous K block is promoted (weight scales loaded one K block earlier,
  activation scales from its still-owned smem_sfa stage) and its empty
  barrier is arrived.
- L2: each per-64 K half is a unit with the same two-buffer scheme; the low
  half of K block k overlaps the high half of k-1 being promoted, and a
  stage is released only after both halves are promoted.
- Every unit starts from a zeroed accumulator and units are promoted in the
  original K order (L2 low before high), so outputs must be bit-identical.
- ptxas (CUDA 13.0) serializes every WGMMA of the kernel (C7514) as soon as
  a WGMMA is still in flight at a loop back-edge and its accumulator is read
  afterwards; the first fully rolled version did that and also spilled
  2 KB. The pipeline therefore runs over unrolled chunks (8 K blocks for L1,
  6 for L2) and drains with warpgroup_wait<0> at the end of each chunk, so
  no accumulator is live across a loop back-edge.
- The larger body stopped NVVM from inlining process_math_block and the
  Decode for_each_block callback (C7510 serialization, 6.5 KB spills);
  both lambdas are marked CUTLASS_LAMBDA_FUNC_INLINE. Prefill already
  inlined them: its cubin SASS is byte-identical to the image's.
- ptxas allocates the math region within the 384-thread launch bound (168
  registers) regardless of setmaxnreg.inc, so raising the math budget does
  not help; the candidate stays at 168 and compiles with 0 spills for
  Decode (Prefill unchanged at 40/40 bytes).

Timeline patch rebased on top of the candidate (the full-barrier waits in
the new branch use tl_wait_full) and extended with per-block K-loop time
counters ML1Loop/ML2Loop (block start -> end of K loop, before the
epilogue) so the remaining FC1/FC2 time can be split between mainloop and
epilogue. The summary script lists the two new counters.

Expected check: all 12 cases' output hashes equal 39053820377, Decode
timings improve, and the timeline shows higher weight bandwidth and less
loader slot wait.
