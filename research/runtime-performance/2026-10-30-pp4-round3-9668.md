## 3. Where 10k stands

- Prefill sets the limit. Capacity is 3 × 2 / 117.4 ms, about 51.1 req/s; the run delivered 49.4 req/s.
- With the current Prefill, the round-3 model caps Decode-only gains at about 9,710.
- 10,000 needs about 4% less Prefill work per period, about 4.7 ms of 117 ms.

Per 24-layer stage (40825417205 profile and the round-3 MegaMoE accounting):

| kernel | per stage | efficiency at the measured clock |
|---|---|---|
| MegaMoE | about 63 ms | 59–60% of FP8 peak; mainloop bound by the 128 B/clk shared-memory port (r3m) |
| FA3 prefill | about 20 ms | 39–44% of FP8 peak |
| QKV + O | about 21 ms | 81–87% of FP8 peak |

The overlap designs for MegaMoE all lost on measurement:

- ping-pong
- independent warpgroups
- FC2 interleave
- direct FC2
- A gather

What remains is to remove shared-memory bytes per MAC in the MegaMoE mainloop, and to raise the efficiency of FA3 prefill.
