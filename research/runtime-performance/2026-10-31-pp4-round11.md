## 1. Gate result

- The gate took 35–36 s per worker, cold JIT included, inside its 120 s bound. Slurm job 27789 completed in 10:39 (round 10: 9:25).
- **Numerics and endurance passed on all 12 GPUs.** At M 3994 and 7988, all five outputs (Q, contiguous K/V, paged K/V) were no worse than fp32 against the FP64 reference, before and after 300 back-to-back calls. The direct-register epilogue is correct and does not hang.
- **Speed failed on every GPU**, with the same ratio forward and reverse.

| M | stock GEMM | fp32 post | GEMM + post | fused | fused / (GEMM + post) |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 7988 | 482–507 µs | 78–81 µs | 561–583 µs | 741–775 µs | 1.32–1.34 |
| 3994 | 232–246 µs | 39–40 µs | 268–287 µs | 363–386 µs | 1.33–1.35 |

Ranges are per-GPU medians over the 12 GPUs.

- Fused is about 190 µs slower than GEMM + post at M 7988, and 260 µs slower than the GEMM alone. Removing the BF16 round trip saves at most the 79 µs post, so the fused kernel spends about 270 µs in an epilogue that the stock GEMM overlaps or does not have.
  - That is consistent with an epilogue that runs serially after each tile's mainloop with no overlap across the persistent tiles, plus fewer pipeline stages at 216448 B of shared memory. This was not profiled.
- It matches the earlier shared-memory fusion on H100 (326.55 against 293.41 µs, 2026-10-24). **Both ways of fusing norm/RoPE/FP8 into the 1D2D QKV epilogue have now lost.** Do not retry this shape of fusion without first showing an epilogue that overlaps the next tile's mainloop.
