## 1. The failure of 40849459077

PP1 of prefill-1 and prefill-2 segfaulted in `cuStreamWaitEvent`
(`_qpp_recv_proxy` → `forward_stream.wait_event`, validation path) on the first
warmup transfer. The receiver had opened the sender's interprocess events with
`Event.from_ipc_handle(peer_device, h)`, i.e. in the sender device's context,
and waited on them from its own device's stream. The fix opens them on the
receiving stage's own device (the pattern of CUDA's simpleIPC). Because a
segfault escapes the in-process validation/vote, the copy engine became opt-in
(`SGLANG_PP4_PROXY_TRANSPORT`, default nccl) behind an in-job probe
(`scripts/check-firmus-pp4-copy-engine.py`, four Decode GPUs of the tail node,
12 transfers with slot reuse and GPU-sleep-delayed copies).

In 40883389891 the probe passed on all four ranks (0 mismatches), and every
stage of all three pipelines logged `PP4 proxy transport: copy (this stage ok
after 16 validated launches)`.

## 4. Where 10k stands

Prefill capacity is 3 pipelines x 2 requests per period: about 49.6 req/s at
121 ms. The run delivered 48.0 req/s, so Prefill is ~97% busy and caps the
score near 9,700 even without the start-burst TTFT failures (127 = 2.1%).
10,000 needs a Prefill period of about 112-115 ms (-5..8%) with the burst
failures reduced, or more Prefill throughput per GPU. Levers that remain:
adopting the bit-exact four-stage MegaMoE (-1.5 ms, rule fix below), and
kernel-level Prefill work (MegaMoE is ~55% of a stage period).

Next rule change: adopt a bit-exact MegaMoE variant on its total saving over
all GPUs and routings and drop the per-GPU sum check, because one GPU's two
cases vary by about +-100 us between runs (GPU 3: +108 us, then -33 us).
