## 1. The hang of 40991744289

Selections adopted in that run:

- Prefill MegaMoE four-stage-lean-split.
- Prefill FA3 `n192-direct-o-varlen-pipe`.
- Decode MoE input fused.
- Decode QKV BLOCK_M 192 × N80.

How the hang unfolded:

- AIPerf warmup got 77 replies.
- All three Prefill workers then stopped issuing work within 2 s of each other, about 13 microbatches in.
- Their KV pools were full: 2944 of 131072 tokens free, 10 in flight.
- Decode waited on about 50 transfers per rank, and the watchdog fired 300 s later.
- No CUDA error was raised, and every GPU went to idle power.

How 41005872470 split the two suspects:

| suspect | what 41005872470 did | result |
|---|---|---|
| FA3 pipe row | removed | — |
| MegaMoE lean-split | in-job endurance gate | passed: 600 consecutive calls per GPU on all 12 GPUs in 5.8 s, last outputs bit-equal to control for balanced, hot and edges |

Serving then ran normally: 6212 requests, no stall.

The cause is therefore the FA3 pipe row, under serving conditions. Its code review found no hang path. The Genkai endurance probe 41002136950 (9 shapes, 2,250 calls) is queued. The pipe row stays in the image and in `prefill-candidates.json`; it is not screened in the PP4 job until it is fixed.

Lesson: an in-job selection makes about 30 calls per GPU and cannot catch a hang that appears after a few hundred calls. A kernel that has not served before needs an endurance gate before serving.
