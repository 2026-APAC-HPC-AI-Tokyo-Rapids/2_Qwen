## 1. Gate result

- **Adopted:** `selection.json` says "all twelve H200 GPUs passed numerics/endurance and saved at least 5 us in both orders on every pair". There were 12 distinct UUIDs and one run ID.
- **Time:** each GPU took 21.8–38.6 s, cold JIT included, inside the 150 s bound. Slurm job 27884 completed in 10:52 (round 10: 9:25, round 11: 10:39). READY came after 367 s.
- **Numerics:** at 3994+3994 the candidate's relative L2 against the FP32 causal reference was 0.026, against 0.017 for the served N192 row. The bound is 0.05. The max scaled error was 0.032–0.036, bound 0.25.
  - Endurance: after 1200 calls in a row through the serving path, the FP8 O and the FP32 scale were byte-equal to the candidate's first output.
- **Speed:** the timed unit is producer plus FA3 per call. The saving is the per-GPU median of control minus candidate, over the forward and reverse orders.
