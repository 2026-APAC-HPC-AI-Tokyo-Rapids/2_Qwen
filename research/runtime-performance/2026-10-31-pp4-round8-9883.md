- **The count under 3 s rose only from 107 to 109.** After the first token, the burst drains at the
  Prefill rate:
  - three workers × 2 requests per ~115 ms period ≈ 52 requests per second
  - so about (3 s − intercept) × 52 ≈ 109 requests meet the SLA
- **The burst costs about 2% of the score.** At 6241 requests, 121 failures are 1.9%. Without
  them the score would be about 10,080.
- **What would reduce it:**

    first Decode step): every 0.1 s is about 5 requests (+0.08%).
  - **More Prefill rate**: the same lever as the steady state.
