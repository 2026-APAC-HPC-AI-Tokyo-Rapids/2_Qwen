# PP4 round 12 survey: what remains between 9896 and 10k

Analysis only, 2026-10-31. Base `7b1dd68` (`origin/main`); branch `perf/pp4-r12-survey-20261031`. No cluster jobs, workflow dispatches, SSH, image builds, source changes or experiment pushes were performed. The only push for this task is the survey commit on this branch, with CI skipped.

## Decision

The most defensible next candidate is **M192/N96 FA3 for two-request varlen calls only, with the served M128/N192 row retained for single requests**. An existing, previously unrecorded-on-main screen already measured **17.86–27.25 us saved per two-request layer call on all 12 H200s**, equivalent to **0.43–0.65 ms per 24-layer stage before serving uncertainty**. It never served: one *single-sequence* cell missed the existing 5 us gate. Do not lower that gate or describe the existing candidate as adopted. A new shape-scoped dispatch must pass the gate for every geometry it actually supports, plus endurance.

That is about **0.35–0.55% of the 118.1 ms period**, not the full 1.1–1.2% needed. The two next research directions are a **once-per-V-tile N192 layout** and **FA3 sum-chain scheduling while retaining the current max/EX2 algorithm**. Neither has a GPU-validated implementation or a reliable positive point estimate. Bundle them only if CPU compilation demonstrates the specific work reduction below; otherwise bundle only the first candidate. There is no evidence-backed package that guarantees 10k.

The required saving is about **1.3–1.4 ms per stage period** across all three pipelines. Exact arithmetic from the best and its repeat gives `118.1 * (1 - score / 10000)` = **1.227 ms** at 9896.09 and **1.397 ms** at 9881.67. A 0.3% period lever must save **0.354 ms**, or **14.8 us/layer** at 24 layers. Optimizing only prefill-2 affects roughly one third of aggregate Prefill capacity; all-worker changes are assumed below.

There are 45 complete target microbatches per stage, with 24/24/24/22 MegaMoE and QKV calls per microbatch.

CUDA ordinals in the kernel table are PP stages 0/1/2/3. **Do not join `deviceId` directly to the physical GPU index**: `TARGET_INFO_CUDA_DEVICE` maps CUDA ordinals 0/1/2/3 to GPU IDs 0/1/3/2, matching the worker's `gpu=0,1,3,2` line. Clip **every kernel on every stream** to that interval; preserve sums and subtract the union overlap once. Boundaries include the next batch's input-norm tail. The layer table uses the same cyclic boundary convention, so a row's suffix norm normally prepares the next layer. This is an accounting interval, not an assertion that the norm belongs semantically to the preceding layer.

## 2. Floors, power and estimate conventions

For a **conditional** H200 scenario at 1.40 GHz: FP8 peak = `132 × 8192 × 1.40e9` = **1.514 PFLOP/s**, BF16 peak half that; HBM reference bandwidth 4.8 TB/s; one-direction NVLink reference 450 GB/s. These are the existing theory model's definitions, not measured kernel rates. No H100 timing is converted to H200 speed. The raw GPC series in nsys is labelled MHz despite values of order 1e9; physical mapping and kernel-local clocks are not established well enough here to use it as an exact per-kernel floor.

Sources and formulas: `kernel-theory-model.json`, `2026-10-30-prefill-megamoe-ep1-accounting.md`, `2026-10-31-prefill-dense-pp4.md`, `2026-10-30-prefill-fa3-diag-trim.md` and the actual onepass source patch. There is no claim that the sum of these independent resource bounds is an attainable layer time.

Saving convention below: **positive S means a shorter period**; negative S means a regression. Ranges are engineering envelopes, **not confidence intervals**. “If successful” ranges describe the branch that passes a gate. The gate can also return zero adoption. Removed FLOPs/bytes/instructions use power factor **kappa approximately 1**; removing dependency waits or idle uses **0.36–0.6**. Where the resource's energy share is unknown, state that assumption rather than multiplying a whole kernel by the removed byte fraction.

At a 700 W cap, **less work in one kernel can let that GPU's clock rise**, including during adjacent kernels if the power-control averaging window spans them. It can also simply consume less energy during a shorter interval. There is no measured per-kernel power-control response curve here. The period benefit of the saved work and the associated clock change are the **same energy saving**, not two additive benefits. Conversely, filling idle time raises average activity and can lower clocks, explaining the smaller idle factor. Existing evidence: after NCCL idle was removed, g1:0 clock fell about 1–1.5%; after split, the period shortened less than its work reduction while clock fell about 1%. No node-wide power sharing exists between Prefill and Decode GPUs. Raising the cap, locking a higher clock, or moving Decode's power budget to Prefill is not a remaining work-removal lever.

## 3. Ranked live levers and explicit boundaries

### 1. Restrict M192/N96 to two-request varlen FA3 — first choice

- **Shape / precision:** two full sequences around L3994, also ragged pairs; Q64/KV4/D128, same E4M3 Q/K/V and group-128 FP8 O, FP32 softmax/accumulation. Single requests retain the served M128/N192 row. Scope is request-count/geometry based, never benchmark time or SLA based.
- **Work:** at L4001, existing tile census changes M-tiles 2004→1336, KV visits 21924→28560 (more smaller visits), logical K+V tile loads **1.07761→0.70189 GB/sequence**, tile tensor work **275.87→269.53 GFLOP**. Unique input and useful FLOPs are unchanged; NVLink bytes unchanged. More online-softmax/output-rescale updates partly offset the saved traffic. Do not call the 34.9% logical tile-load reduction a 34.9% attention gain or a measured HBM reduction.
- **Floor:** unchanged useful tensor bound, about 0.345 ms per two-request layer in the 1.40 GHz scenario. The tile tensor floor at L4001 is about 182.2→178.0 us/sequence, only **4.2 us** lower; most potential gain is traffic/scheduling, not that FLOP difference.
- **Prior attempts / difference:** M192/N128 spilled and serialized WGMMA (C7512). M192/N96 compiled in CUDA 13.0.88 with **128 launch registers, 40/28 B spill stores/loads**, versus control 168 and 84/116 B, no C7512; math WG budget 160. Existing changes are `821f409`/`fd8a66c`. In **41151283746** all 48 screen cells passed numerics, but one single-L4027 cell saved **4.546 us**, below the unchanged 5 us rule. Control served; M192 endurance did not run. New proposal is **a separate two-request dispatch contract**, not another unchanged all-shape screen or a relaxed threshold. Single-sequence regressions/failures cannot reach this dispatch.
- **Evidence / saving:** 41151283746's two-request `3986+4027` cells save **17.858–27.255 us/layer**, mean **23.176**, on all twelve GPUs. `24 × saving` = **0.429–0.654 ms/period** as a gate-equivalent projection. This pair is close to, but not identical to, actual 3994+3994. Allow **S = 0.25–0.70 ms** in serving if adopted (working expectation **0.4–0.6 ms**, about **0.35–0.5%**); zero if the new shape/endurance gate fails. No additional clock multiplier: the H200 screen already includes its own clock response, but serving transfer remains uncertain.
- **Implementation / build:** small-to-medium integration, approximately 100–200 lines of dispatch, shape-contract and gate changes on top of the existing M192 native row, plus fixtures. **Fresh image required from the round-10 base**, compiling the FA3 companion row and carrying the precise SGLang dispatch overlay. Do not replace the base wholesale with the unrelated sparse/graph bundle image.
- **Gate / risk:** all 12 GPUs, actual 3994+3994 as well as 3986+4027, 3986+3986, 4027+4027, 4096+4096 and ragged pairs within supported bounds; existing FP32/reference tolerances, changed-input replay, control comparisons and at least the existing 5 us/call margin, followed by **1200 serving-path calls** and deterministic bytes against the candidate's own numerically approved output. Keep exact contract on unchanged paths. Existing M192 reference L2 is roughly 0.026–0.029 versus control 0.016–0.017: within that screen's policy but numerically different, so no bit-equality or quality-equivalence claim. Ordinary output/eligibility checks remain necessary. Risk **medium**: unserved row, reduction-order change, endurance unknown, one near-threshold single geometry already closed.

### 2. Produce the N192 V layout once, instead of transposing on every visit

- **Shape:** same two-sequence N192 FA3 as the served pin; FP8 V, 4 heads × L × 128, separate request boundaries. The QK writer still produces the required paged KV unchanged.
- **Work:** producer currently performs LDSM.T/PRMT/STSM for V on about **21,924 visits/sequence** at L4001. Eliminating that round trip removes approximately **48 KiB × 21,924 = 1.078 GB of shared-memory traffic/sequence**, plus transpose/permutation instructions. Unique V is only about **2.05 MB**. The K+V TMA loads and attention FLOPs do **not** disappear. Write the native consumer layout instead of the existing contiguous V representation where possible, rather than add a redundant full buffer pass.
- **Floor:** useful tensor/EX2 floor unchanged; transpose contribution can approach zero but attention cannot approach zero. Exact producer energy share is **unknown**. A conditional 1.5–4% of the roughly 20 ms attention budget corresponds to **0.3–0.8 ms** gross energy benefit (kappa about 1 for removed work). Charge **0–0.3 ms** extra QK-writer/scatter/layout cost per period. Working **S = 0–0.5 ms**; broad envelope **−0.4 to +0.8 ms**. This is a plausible >=0.3% opportunity, not a demonstrated positive gain.
- **Prior / difference:** old N224 `VByteImage` was not correct. `e3e7744` explains that an old thread-local LDSM/STSM composition was wrong on **27,776/28,672 bytes/tile** and documents cross-lane repair; later accounting still records a failed dump check. Do not reuse its CPU-only map as validated. Proposed work is **N192-specific**, independently generates the deployed instruction layout, respects varlen request boundaries, and requires a GPU byte-image dump **before** attention timing. ### 3. Shorten FA3's FP32 sum recurrence without changing max or exponential

- **Shape:** current M128/N192 pipe (or explicitly composed M192/N96), Q64/KV4/D128 at L~4K. Same EX2, all scores and probabilities, same FP8 conversions. Reassociate FP32 additions into independent chains and interleave the two rows; no approximate exp, no lower precision, no omitted attention terms.
- **Work:** this is **dependency/idle removal**, not a FLOP saving. The N192 census has **96 FADD** and 98 EX2 per thread per steady step; roughly 48-element row reductions have long recurrences. Four independent sum chains can shorten one row's critical addition depth from roughly 48 to roughly 14, at the cost of merges and extra live state. The exact emitted depth must be demonstrated in SASS, not assumed from source.
- **Floor / estimate:** Tensor and EX2 bounds unchanged. An illustrative exposed **100–150 cycles** removed from a **2936-cycle** steady step is 3–5% of that step; apply idle factor **0.36–0.6** to at most ~20 ms of attention: gross **0.2–0.6 ms**, before merge/register costs. Working **S = 0–0.4 ms**; broad envelope **−0.6 to +0.6 ms**. The 2936-cycle stamp is an **H100 diagnostic from 40981460928**, not an H200 time. It supports a dependency question, not an absolute H200 saving.
- **Prior / difference:** `3c8dfec` tree-**max** shortened max dependency depth 50→16 at N192 but lost **1.2–6%**; it deliberately preserved the sum chain. This proposal leaves **max unchanged** and changes only the sum recurrence/schedule, preserving PV operand lifetimes. Half2 exp (two MUFUs plus conversions), polynomial exp (+5.2%), lazy-rescale (below margin) and the diagonal-only softmax trim (+~1%) remain rejected. Given the max result, the prior probability of a win is modest.
- **Implementation / build:** small-to-medium, approximately 100–200 lines including SASS attribution and a distinct row/gate; **FA3 image rebuild required**. CPU acceptance requires fewer exposed sum-dependency cycles with no new C75xx serialization, no extra consumer spills, unchanged existing-row SASS and a demonstrated late PV operand read.
- **Gate / risk:** changed FP order requires reference tolerance and changed-input/endurance checks rather than old-byte equality. Time complete FA3/O path on all twelve GPUs. Risk **high**: longer live ranges, compiler wait motion, FP8 rounding differences; could reproduce the tree-max loss despite looking shorter in source.
