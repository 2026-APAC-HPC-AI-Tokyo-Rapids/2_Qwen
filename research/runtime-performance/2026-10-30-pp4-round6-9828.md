# PP4 round 6: 9828.95 tok/s, every new kernel fell back (2026-10-30)

Branch `perf/pp4-r6-20261030`. Serving commit 5022b5b, with build and serving in one run.
Topology P3-TP1-PP4 / D1-TP4-DP4-EP4. Concurrency 232, Decode cap 48 per DP rank.

| run | what | result |
|---|---|---|
| 41020974704 | build + PP4 serving of 5022b5b | 9828.95 (official parser valid), Slurm 15:32 |

All three new kernels lost their in-job gates. The serving configuration was therefore the one in 41005872470 (9832.74). This run is effectively a repeat of it.

## 1. Gates

| candidate | gate result | expected |
|---|---|---|
| MegaMoE four-stage-lean-split-ldsm | 62394 µs total against split 61421 µs. It is **+1.6% slower**: about +40 µs per call on every GPU. Split stayed | −0.75..−1.25 ms per period |
| combine fused with the next add-RMSNorm | Numerics passed (residual bit-equal, relative L2 9e-5). Time was **slower in 22 of 24 cases**, by 0 to +28 µs, median about +15 µs. Not adopted | −24..−33 µs per layer |
| Decode FA3 h4 stream split, `p32stream-h4-fp8` | The selector printed no record within its 175 s timeout. The h2 screen in 41005872470 finished in 16.5 s. rc 124, the pin stayed | −0.56 ms per step |

What each result means:

- **split-ldsm.** The swizzled ldmatrix path did not shorten the mainloop. The cost model of round 5 assumed that A-fragment loads set the K-block time. Round 3 had already shown that the FP32 carried-scale rescale sets it. The measurement agrees with round 3.
- **combine-norm.** The gate times the combine plus the next layer's add-RMSNorm, fused against separate. The extra per-token reduction inside the combine costs more than the separate norm launch and its read of the hidden state. The cause within the kernel was not profiled.
- **h4.** No record was printed before the timeout, so the h4 row either hangs or takes more than 150 s for its timing plus 301 replays of 94 layers per batch. The previous screen finished in 16.5 s, so a hang is the likely reading. The timeout and fallback kept it out of serving. It cost about 160 s of the job, and the Slurm time rose from 12:39 to 15:32.

## 3. Consequences

- The MegaMoE mainloop is bound by the rescale. A further gain there has to remove FP32 rescale work per K block, not shared-memory traffic.
- Remove combine-norm from the screen.
- Do not screen h4 again until a 1-GPU endurance run has localised the hang.
- The FA3 prefill pipe hardening (r6f) is still unscreened, pending the Genkai endurance probe 41002136950.
