every setting sat in very long one-line commands, constants were repeated

  named groups, one table of workers driving the endpoint file, the launch
  loop and readiness, and sections in job order.
- submission/run/prepare.sh, submission/basic/prepare.sh: named revisions

  job scripts), comments on why each step exists.
